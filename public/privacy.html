What Pdash stores

Last updated 11 November 2026. Plain English, because you should be able to read it.

The short version. If you make an account, what you log is saved on a server and the person who runs this app can read it. If that is not okay, use it without an account — then nothing leaves your device.

Without an account

Everything stays in your browser's local storage on that one device. Nothing is uploaded, there is no tracking, no analytics and no third party involved. Clearing your browser data erases it, so use Export Backup in Profile if you care about it.

With an account

These are stored on Supabase, the hosting service this app uses:

  • Your email address, and a securely hashed password — never the password itself.
  • Your training data: workouts, sets, weights, reps, reps-in-reserve and personal records.
  • Your meals and goals, including calories and macros if you log them.
  • Your profile: display name, level, XP, streaks, and your chosen theme.
  • The date you agreed to this, and the wording you agreed to.

Who can see it

You, on any device you sign in to. And the person who runs this app, who can read the accounts and training data described above in order to operate and improve it.

Other users cannot see your data. The database enforces that with row-level security, not just a rule in the app's code. Your data is not sold, and it is not shared with advertisers or any other third party.

Deleting it

Profile → Delete account removes your save, your profile and your login. It is immediate and cannot be undone, so export a backup first if you want to keep a copy.

Keeping it safe

Traffic is encrypted in transit, and access is controlled per account at the database level. No system is perfect: do not store anything here you could not bear to lose or to have exposed. This is a personal project, not a company with a security team.

Children

Not intended for under-13s. Please do not create an account if you are younger than that.

Changes

If what is collected or who can see it changes, this page is updated and you will be asked to agree again. What you previously agreed to is kept on record and is not rewritten.
